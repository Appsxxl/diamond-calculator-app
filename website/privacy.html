Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Plan B ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Xelo Media. This policy explains how we handle your personal data when you use the Plan B mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only your email address, provided voluntarily when you sign in using our magic-link authentication system. We do not collect your name, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your email address is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and maintain your session
  • Send you a one-time sign-in code when you request it
  • Identify your account within the app

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate the app:

  • Resend (resend.com) — for sending authentication emails
  • Railway (railway.app) — for secure server hosting and data storage

These services process data only as necessary to deliver their function and are bound by their own privacy policies.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your email address is stored securely on our servers hosted via Railway with encrypted connections. We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your data is removed within 30 days.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise these rights, contact us at: stigapp@xelo.media

6. Children's Privacy

Plan B is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify users of significant changes via the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
